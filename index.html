    How to join

    Connect from Minecraft Pocket Edition in three steps.

    1. Open MinecraftTap Play, then open the Servers tab.
    2. Add the serverEnter a name, then the address and port 19132.
    3. Connect and playTap the server. Ranks appear automatically after purchase.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the server free to join?

    Yes. Every player can join and play for free. Store items are optional.

    When do I get my rank?

    Ranks are applied to your in-game name right after your purchase is confirmed.

    Which devices can connect?

    Any device running Minecraft Pocket Edition or Bedrock, including phones, tablets and consoles that support custom servers.
